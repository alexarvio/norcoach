With your clients

  • Chat

    One thread per client, beside their plan. Your WhatsApp is yours again.

  • A link on any message

    A message can point at the exercise, meal or photo it’s about, so nobody has to ask which one.

  • Video review

    Ask for a video on any exercise. Slow it down, draw on it and talk over it, on camera if you like.

  • Meetings

    One-off or repeating calls with prep notes, so you walk in prepared. They leave with a summary.

Their progress

  • Goals until the next call

    Up to three goals from each call, so they know what to work on.

  • Shape updates

    Progress photos from the angles you ask for. Side by side, they show the change the scales miss.

  • Events

    A trip, an illness, a busy week. You or your client add it, so you plan around it.

  • Health data, read for you

    Steps, weight and sleep from Apple Health or Health Connect. Nothing to type in.

Your business

  • Needs you

    Every message, video, note and call request in one list, so you see who’s waiting on you.

  • Invoices

    One-off or repeating, from your own price plans. Billing runs itself, and you see who owes.

  • Your business in numbers

    Who joins, who leaves and what you earn, month by month.

  • Calendar

    Your calls and sessions on one calendar, with double-bookings flagged.

And

  • Strength, worked out

    PBs, trends and next week’s weights from every set. No sums by hand.

  • Your exercise library

    Your exercises and demo videos, filmed once, for every client.

  • On your phone

    Your chats, video reviews and calendar, wherever you are.

  • Your logo

    Behind every screen, so the app feels like yours.
