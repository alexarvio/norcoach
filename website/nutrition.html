Nutrition

Targets for training and rest days, and a food diary built in.

See what your client really ate, right next to their training, without asking for screenshots from another app.

On your laptop

  1. 1

    Set the targets

    Set their calories and macros separately for training days and rest days, so they eat more on the days they train.

  2. 2

    Add supplements

    List what they should take and when. They tick each one off as they go, so you never have to ask.

  3. 3

    See what they ate

    See each macro against your target, based on what they actually logged rather than what they remember, and leave a comment on any meal.

On their phone

  1. 1

    The day as a ring

    Their calories and macros fill up as rings through the day, so they can always see what’s left.

  2. 2

    A food diary they’ll use

    The food library is full of everyday foods, and they can save their usual meals, so logging a normal day takes very little time.

  3. 3

    Or just the number

    On a busy day they can simply type in their calories, or let Norcoach read them from Apple Health or Health Connect.

In phases

A nutrition phase is a set of targets.

A cut, a lean bulk or maintenance each runs for the number of weeks you give it, so your client knows exactly how long the hard part lasts.

On the first Monday of the next phase, their targets change by themselves, and you can compare one phase with the next.
