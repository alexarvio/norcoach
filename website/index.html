I was Finlay’s client before we built anything. He’s a great coach, but he ran his business across a handful of different tools, and at some point he started to lose control of it a little. The coaching stopped feeling as personal as one-to-one should.

Features

Built for one-to-one coaching.

  • Video feedback

    Slow their lift down, draw on it and talk them through it. They see you did it yourself.

  • Every message linked

    Tie a message to the exercise, meal or check-in it’s about. Your client taps it and lands right on it, so nothing gets lost in a long chat.

FAQ

Questions coaches ask.

Who is Norcoach for?

One-to-one online and hybrid coaches who write each client’s plan themselves, and want to take on more clients without the coaching getting worse. It isn’t for selling one programme to hundreds of people.

I already use a coaching app. Why switch?

Most coaching apps are built to handle more people, and more of them now let AI write your replies and plans. Norcoach is built so each client still feels coached by you: everything they read comes from you, and one list shows you who needs you today.

How do I move my clients over?

You don’t do it alone. We get on a call with you and bring your clients and their programmes in together. Then each client joins with your code or a link you send them.

Do my clients need to download anything?

Just the Norcoach app. From the first day they get a proper app with your name on it, instead of a shared spreadsheet and a chat thread.

Will my clients notice the difference?

Yes, from the first day. They can see where they are in their plan and what comes next, and every reply comes from you, never from a bot. That’s the coaching people stay for, and tell their friends about.

Does Norcoach use AI?

No. Nothing your clients read is written by AI. Norcoach only does the sums for you, like PBs and next week’s weights.

Can a client lose a workout?

No. Every set is saved on their phone the moment it’s entered, even with no signal in the gym, and reaches you once they’re back online.

Who answers when I need help?

The people who build Norcoach. Write to us from Support in the app, and you’ll hear back from us, not a bot.

What does it cost?

Nothing while we’re in early access. We’ll tell you the price before you ever pay, and every feature is included.

Is my clients’ data mine?

Yes. Only you and your clients can see it, and you can export all of it whenever you like.
