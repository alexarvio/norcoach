How it works

Phases

Plan every client in phases.

A phase is a block of weeks with one aim, such as a strength block, a cut or a better sleep routine. Your client always sees the phase they’re in and which week it is, such as week 4 of 6, and their events show when the next one starts.

1

Build it

Choose the track, give the phase a name and a number of weeks, then fill it in. It stays a draft that only you can see, so you can plan ahead whenever it suits you.

2

Schedule it

Give it a start date or make it live straight away. When one phase ends, the next takes over by itself, so no client is ever left waiting for their next block.

3

Let it run

Your client’s app shows the phase they’re in and which week it is. You can change anything along the way, or let a phase run with no end date.

Where a phase stands

Always one clear state.

  • Draft

    Only you can see it, so you can build it in your own time.

  • Scheduled

    Its dates are set, and it starts by itself on its first Monday.

  • Live

    Your client is in it now, and their app shows which week they’re on.

  • Past

    It has ended, and everything logged in it, every set, meal and check-in, is kept.

Your client sees the phase they’re in, never the plan behind it.

Events

Real life, on the same timeline.

When your client has a trip, a holiday or a busy week at work, either of you can add it, and it sits under their phases, so you plan around it instead of hearing about it afterwards.

A weight goal sits there too, with the path to reach it and whether your client is on track.
