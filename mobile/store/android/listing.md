## App name (30 characters at most)

Norcoach

## Short description (80 characters at most)

Your coach’s plan, workouts, check-ins and chat, together in one app.

## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

Norcoach is the app your coach uses to coach you one to one. Everything your coach plans for you, and everything you send back, lives in one place.

Your plan, in phases
Your coaching runs in phases for your training, your nutrition and your lifestyle. You always see the phase you’re in and which week it is, and your events show when the next one starts.

Train from your programme
Open today’s session to find your coach’s notes and targets on every exercise, with what you lifted last time beside each set, so you always know what to beat. Every set you log is kept, even with no signal in the gym. When your coach asks, film a set and send it for feedback.

Eat to your targets
Your calories and macros fill up as rings through the day. Log your meals from a food library, add a photo of the plate, and save the meals you eat often so they’re one tap away next time.

Check in within a few taps
Your check-in waits for you on your home screen. Connect Health Connect, and your steps, weight and sleep fill in by themselves.

Talk to your coach
Message your coach, and send photos, videos and voice messages. Their replies, notes and video reviews all come from them, never from AI.

You join Norcoach with your coach’s code. Only you and your coach can see your data.
