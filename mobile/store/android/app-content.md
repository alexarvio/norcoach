Collected, for App functionality and Account management, not optional unless said:
- Personal info: Name, Email address, Phone number (optional), Address (optional), Other info: date of birth (optional)
- Health and fitness: Health info, Fitness info (check-ins, weight, sleep, steps, heart rate; workouts and meals)
- Photos and videos: Photos, Videos (progress photos, meal pictures, exercise videos; optional)
- Audio: Voice or sound recordings (voice messages; optional)
- Messages: Other in-app messages (the chat with the coach)
- Files and docs (files sent in the chat; optional)
- App activity: Other user-generated content (notes, check-in answers)
- Device or other IDs (the push notification token)

Not collected: location, financial or payment info, contacts, calendar, web browsing, app info and performance.
