# Norcoach client app · design rules

Read this before touching any client screen. One hero language, one everyday language, nothing else. (The coach admin has its own: `docs/DESIGN-RULES-ADMIN.md`.)

## 1. Two languages, no third
- **Hero**: the phase cards on Home only. Full-bleed photo, dark glass panel, white type. Nothing else may look like this, with one exception: the food diary's meal cards (9 Nov, Alex; docs/boards/meal-cards-mockup.html, option A; §9). A card is the phase on now and never names the one coming next, nor does a "No plan yet" card (3 Nov, Alex: "that is something we show on events").
- **Hairline**: everything else. Thin 1px lines, rings and icons alone, light type, the page colour behind. No white cards with borders and drop shadows, no gradient fills, no tiles around icons.

**Where hairline belongs (16 Oct, Alex):** screens you read and scroll: lists, feeds, timelines (Home, Events, Meetings, Notifications, the session overview under its photo header, and the workout screen: sets on one spine, the open set as big light numerals with a thin underline instead of a box; the Training tab, shape updates, settings, the check-in screen, the food diary, Nutrition, invoices and, lightly, chat too). Pop-ups and sheets where you enter things (the check-in, measurements, Add an event, Request a meeting, the gym picker) keep their fields and boxes; don't flatten them. Since 17 Oct they speak the shared voice: a plain white sheet (no wash or glow), titles at 600, small grey uppercase labels, fields and tiles white with a hairline (ink while typing, 1px: 18 Nov, Alex, a 1.5px edge rounds to uneven sides on an iPhone's 3× screen), the chosen choice ink, the thing's colour only on its icon, ring or edge, one ink pill to save. A pop-up that is only a list to pick one from is flat rows instead (16 Nov, Alex: B of docs/boards/pick-popup-mockup.html): no box round a row, space between them, the icon in its track's colour, the name at 16px/500 with its fact grey under it, a chevron after (Add a session and its Duplicate which?, Add a meal); forms keep their boxes.

**The one exception (16 Oct, by choice):** the Training tab's week card and its session cards keep their card shape (the done tint does real work on a to-do list). They speak the shared tokens: 500/600 type, the palette indigo and green, tints at 8–12%, a white hairline chip for Up next. Do not restyle them to hairline rows; that was tried and reverted.

## 2. Page and lines
- Page `#F5F5F5` on Home, `#F5F6F8` on the pushed screens (Events, Workout, Notifications). One per screen, never two tones on one screen.
- Home's top is the website's dark (21 Nov, Alex: A of docs/boards/home-dark-arc-mockup.html, as the apply page and the sign-up on a phone): the top bar and the greeting on `#1c1c1e`, the bar white over it and the clock with it, the date in the dark's grey and the greeting white; the paper rises over the dark's foot with 28px round top corners and holds the rest of Home from Today down. The logo's star is the dark's own, white and very faint (5%), cropped off the top right at the spot it always had (`--mark-y`), so it scrolls away with the dark (21 Nov, Alex: "if I scroll down, I don't see it anymore"); the paper under it is plain, no star. With a brand colour (§4) the dark is that colour, darkened as always until white reads on it, the date a see-through white (21 Nov, Alex: "if I change the branding color… that black card now… also changes"). A coach's own logo is shown whole, never cut to a corner (21 Nov, Alex: "it shows like 25% of that"; A of docs/boards/coach-logo-whole-mockup.html): on the dark's right from just under the clock to the arc, white at 14%, behind the bar's name and the bell (`has-coach-logo` on the body, app/layout.tsx). Scrolled, the bar is the paper's, as on Training and Nutrition (the Home tab's `darkBanner`; "Home under the dark arc" at the end of globals.css). Was paper from the top down (24 Oct, Alex: mockup A, docs/boards/home-background-mockup.html).
- Every chat is paper with the logo's star huge and very faint (4.5% ink) cropped off the top right, behind everything. It sits at one spot on the screen, the spot it has in Home's dark, so going from Home to a chat it never moves (17 Nov, Alex and Finlay: it "clicks a little bit"; `MarkAnchor`, `--mark-y`), nor while the keyboard slides a chat's thread up or down, nor when a message is sent (17 Nov, Alex: in the iPhone app it jumped on send; `--mark-ride`). In a chat it never scrolls (29 Oct, Alex): drawn on the screen's scroll box, never on what scrolls, so it stays where it is while the page moves, in every chat and the coach's admin alike. When the coach has uploaded a logo, it is their logo there instead, as a one-colour shape just as faint, in Home's dark and in the chat alike, and whole (21 Nov, Alex: D of docs/boards/coach-logo-whole-mockup.html): in every chat (the client's, the coach's phone, the laptop's panel) big in the thread's top right with a third of it past the right edge, 300px on a phone and 330px in the laptop's panel (22 Nov, Alex: "the chat background logo needs to be bigger, same idea, 66%"; was 200px and whole, 28 Oct, Alex; docs/DESIGN-RULES-ADMIN.md §2, `--bg-mark`).
- Hairline `#E1E5EB`. Separate rows and sections with **space alone** (17 Oct, Alex): no lines between list rows, no lines over sections or above buttons (3 Nov, Alex: option A of docs/boards/row-lines-mockup.html took out the last ones: the food diary's meals, Settings, the burger menu, Invoices, the check-in's last seven days; a top bar that sticks keeps the page's tone and no line, and so does a dock at the foot: the workout is `#F5F6F8` to its very bottom and behind its dock, its scroll its own, 12 Nov, Alex: "the background would change colour if I kept scrolling down… kind of like the screen cutting"). The only line on a screen is a timeline's spine (Events, the Home events row), and a note's own underline where nothing sits right under it.
- No line under a header, a title or anything that stays on screen as the page scrolls (28 Oct, Alex: the workout's exercise name had a line between it and the coach's note; Home had the same): it is always there, so nothing needs parting from it. Before drawing a line, ask what it parts that space can't; if the answer is a heading or a pinned header, leave it out.
- In the iPhone app the page runs up under the clock (11 Nov, Alex: "could those blend in with the background"): no strip of its own behind the clock and battery, each screen's top taking the clock's room (`html.native-app`, `--sat`, the end of globals.css) so its paper or photo shows behind them, the clock white over a photo or anything dark and ink over the page (`NativeStatusBar`). That room is read once the app is in front and held (17 Nov, Alex: back from the background Home "shifted a tiny bit and went back"; `StableInset`). A phone's browser is as it was.
- White `#fff` only for a pill or a button, and it gets `inset 0 0 0 1px #E1E5EB`, never a shadow.

## 3. Type (Archivo)
- Weights **500** and **600** only. Date numerals may be **300**. Never 700 or above on a client screen.
- Every photo banner (3 Nov, Alex: the audit): the eyebrow at 600, the title at 600 and one size, 26px (a long one shrinks to fit). On one, a strip of facts (the session overview's Date | Gym | Time) is see-through with a soft white edge, its figures white at 600; a count pill (the check-in's 3 / 10) is the banner's glass pill at 600, its dot white until all are in, then green. Nutrition's ring and macro labels are the app's small label, 600, grey.
- Nutrition is the food diary, one screen (12 Nov, Alex: B of docs/boards/nutrition-is-the-diary-mockup.html; the diary behind a tap on the ring was found by nobody): tap Nutrition and you are in today's diary, logging. Its banner exactly the top bar and 231px, the photo and shade, `Nutrition` as the eyebrow, the phase's name as the title (`Your targets` with targets and no phase, `Your nutrition` with neither, the banner grey), the day under it (`Thursday 12 November`), the week strip in the middle of the room left, Training day | Rest day at its foot; a day opens on Training day unless the client picked Rest day on it and logged something there (10 Nov, Alex). The ring and its macros are that day's figures, nothing to tap; a sideways swipe on them is the other kind of day. Leaving the tab and coming back is today, at its top; a link lands on its day and meal (`FoodDiaryScreen`, `FoodFocus`).
- Training's banner is Nutrition's size (21 Nov, Alex: "if I go to nutrition, that expands quite a bit… it just jumps"): the top bar and 231px, its picture laid in the same 420px box from the top, so going between the two tabs the picture is the same size; its words where they were and the week strip at its foot, the extra room above the strip (the "Training's banner as Nutrition's" block at the end of globals.css).
- Eyebrow / label: 10–11px, 600, letter-spacing .16–.18em, uppercase, `#5b6474` or the item's colour.
- Title: 15–17px, 500; the focused or live one 17–18px, 600. One line with an ellipsis, and a chevron when it is cut.
- A pushed screen's own title in its top bar (24 Oct, Alex: the bold ones read too fat, and Shape update's was another size): capitals, Archivo 15px at 600, letter-spacing .16em, ink (white over a photo), centred. One look on every screen the burger and the bell open, Shape update, the check-in and the chat's Media and files (`.cn-title`, `.ci-title`, `.cmg-head`; 24 Oct, Alex: Media and files was still the old design, now the page colour, a plain back arrow, the white hairline capsule for its tabs and files as flat rows with a line icon).
- Secondary: 12px, 500, `#8b93a1`. Tabular numerals for dates, counts and times.
- Dates and times one way (3 Nov, Alex: the audit; docs/boards/dates-times-mockup.html): the day before the month, no commas, `2 Nov`, `Mon 2 Nov`, `Monday 2 November` (British English, `en-GB`, with September short as `Sep`, never `Sept`; never `en-US`'s `Nov 2`). Times 24-hour whatever the phone is set to, `18:00`, `18:00 – 18:30`. Every time is the client's own, in the phone's zone, never with a zone after it (12 Nov, Alex: "it should be assumed that the time in the app is the time the client is in"; a call's time, a notification's `at 09:00 GMT+1` read as the phone's own, `clockToZone`). A day's numeral in a list's date column is two figures at 22px, light (`05`), on Events and Invoices alike.
- Ink `#141a24`, body `#3a4250`, faint `#8b93a1`, label grey `#98A2B3`.
- Every field a client types in is 16px at least (12 Nov, Alex: "the keyboard experience is not the smoothest… it gets in the middle of the text boxes and it lags"): under 16px the phone zooms in on it as it takes the focus, a second jump on top of the keyboard's. A field drawn smaller is raised where it is drawn; a field nothing sizes is caught at the end of globals.css (the keyboard block), in the client app alone, its pop-ups too, never on the coach's side. Pinch-zoom stays allowed: never `maximum-scale`.

  training indigo `#3a3390` · nutrition teal `#1d7a70` · lifestyle beige `#a08a5c` (tint `#efede6`; 21 Oct: one beige everywhere, not amber or grey) · trip `#2f5d8f` · supplement `#1f7a4d` · health `#b8471f` · work `#5b6472` · shape update rust `#b8471f` · meeting blue `#2f6fd6` · weight goal lifestyle beige `#a08a5c` with the target mark (2 Nov, Alex: "I like this color for the weight and also the icon"; `GOAL_COLOR`, `TARGET_ICON` in app/lib/weightGoal.ts).
- One rust and one green (3 Nov, Alex: the audit; docs/boards/colours-mockup.html): rust `#b8471f` for what needs doing, what is behind and every client error (sign-in's too); green `#2f7a3f` for what is done, the check-in's pop-up too. Numbers down a list are ink, never navy (the session overview's as Training's). One amber besides, `#a8761f`, named where it is used and nowhere else: a warm-up (§6), and a video the coach asked for while nothing is sent, its ring, dot and words (12 Nov, Alex: docs/boards/workout-a2-mockup.html, asked amber, sent grey, replied green); an ask is never rust. A swap's mark wears the coach's swap amber (`#9a5b0c`, 13 Nov, Alex), as the admin draws it.
- A colour appears as a thin ring, an icon, a label or a 2px line. Never as a filled block behind text, but for the workout's target band (12 Nov, Alex: its 7% training-purple tint, §6).
- Every line chart one way (4 Nov, Alex; docs/DESIGN-RULES-ADMIN.md §8): one smooth curve through every point (`monotoneCurve`), no dot on each point, the latest a dot pulsing, hovering a faint ink guide, an ink ring and a white hairline pill kept off the line.
- The coach's brand colour (19 Nov, Alex: C of docs/boards/coach-brand-mockup.html, "so it kind of feels that it comes from the coach, not from us"): one colour per coach, any they like (eight swatches, or Custom for any colour, picked or typed as a hex; Settings, Branding), darkened only until white words read on it (4.5:1, its hue kept; `brandColour`, app/lib/brandColour.ts). No hue is refused: the brand only ever fills (19 Nov, Alex: "can we not technically allow the coaches more freedom"), where rust and green are words, rings and lines. It fills the screen's main pill (Start, Save, Join, Finish, Record, Mark as done), the tab you are on, the client's own chat bubbles and Send, Home's dark head (21 Nov, Alex), and tints the faint mark behind Home and the chat, as faint as before; never a toggle, chip, date disc, avatar, text, state, track colour, chart, the sign-in screens or `/coach`. None set, ink as always (`--brand`, `--brand-mark` on `<body>`, the "Coach brand" block at the end of globals.css; a new main pill joins it).
- Tints: `rgba(rgb, .12)` at most. Past things go grey (`grayscale(1)`) and fade to 30–45%; future things keep their colour at ~55%; the live or focused one is at 100%.

## 5. Icons
- The existing category icons, drawn at **stroke 1.6**, round caps and joins, no fills. Icons from different sets in one list draw at one weight (3 Nov, Alex: the burger's, 1.6 on the 24 grid, 1.2 on the 18 grid).
- An icon centred in a ring or a round button sits on whole pixels (28 Oct, Alex): the icon's size and the ring's both even or both odd, so the room either side is whole (a 22px ring, a 12px icon), and the drawing centred in its viewBox.
- The icon alone, in a thin ring: no tile, no disc, no halo. On the Events screen and Home's events timeline every event wears its ring (27 Oct, Alex): one still to come in its category's colour at the row's softer strength, one that is over in grey (`#C9CFD8`), the focused one in full; only what is on today pulses, in its colour.
- A note is marked by a pen writing on a line (4 Nov, Alex: option A of docs/boards/note-icon-mockup.html): one `NoteIcon` (app/components/icons.tsx, `NOTE_ICON_PATHS` where a path is wanted) everywhere, client, coach and phone: Your note, the workout's ⋯ note row, Note to {client}, the Call notes badge. A note is the pen whoever wrote it, the client's own on the coach's screens too (4 Nov, Alex: "pen too"). An Edit stays the pencil; a message in the chat stays the chat icon.
