What changes

  • The Feed is Activity alone: what your clients did. What needs you is the pill's.
  • The pill counts what the Feed's Needs you counts today (messages to answer, videos to review, notes to read, calls asked for, empty live weeks, sign-in locks, join requests); the rail's Feed row keeps the same number.
  • A row opens its thing on that client's tab, as the To do's rows do, and the panel makes way; its action is done in place where it can be (Accept, Decline); the round check lets it go, as now.
  • One panel on the right at a time: the chat, help, a client's To do and Needs you.
Your call: group the rows by kind (Messages, Training…, as the Feed does today), or by client (each client a head with their rows under it)?
