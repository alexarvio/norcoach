Coach app: Settings

Your face at the top right of Chats, Videos and Calendar opens it (today a small sheet with Notifications and Sign out). Only what touches the phone's three jobs: who you are, what buzzes, when it stays quiet, the account. Everything here is one record with the laptop's Settings, so a change on either shows on both. Two ways to lay it out.

A · One screen

A pushed screen with SETTINGS in its top bar, everything on it at once under small caps heads, flat rows on the page, switches as everywhere. Few enough things that nothing needs folding.

Notes
  • On this phone is today's switch (pushes to this device). Messages and Videos are the laptop's two (Settings, Notifications), so turning one off here turns it off there.
  • Quiet hours: the times are a tap each, dropping the quarter-hours list as New entry does; off, they go grey. Clients see "Finlay is away until 07:00" while they hold.
  • The face: a tap opens the phone's own picker to change your photo. Name and business are read here, changed on the laptop.
  • My pick: A. Six switches and a time pair fit on one screen; folding them hides what you came for.
