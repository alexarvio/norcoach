To do as a pop-over

The list opens right under its pill, attached to it, instead of sliding in from the window's right edge where the chat and help live. The same for the Requests pill by Clients.

1 · A client's To do

Under the To do pill, its little point on the pill: the floating lists' look (white, rounded, the soft shadow, no edge), 480 wide, as tall as its rows up to most of the window, then it scrolls inside. Every row: the exercise with its session grey, what happened and the best under it, the chat and the action in one aligned column each; a target not reached opens on its sessions and your own weight. A click outside, Esc or the pill again closes it. The page stays where it is under it.

What changes

  • Nothing slides in from the window's edge for these two any more: the right side stays the chat's and help's.
  • The page never moves for it, and the edge tabs stay where they are.
  • No client name after To do: you are on their screens already.
  • One grid down the list: the chat icons in one column, the actions in the next, their right edges level, whatever the figure.
  • The session goes grey after the exercise's name, so what happened and the best fit on one line, never wrapping.
  • Your own weight: opening a row's sessions ends on Set to and a figure to type, with Set; the suggested one stays the row's action.
