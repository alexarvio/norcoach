Who can get into admin.norcoachapp.com. You and Finlay are owners: every screen and every action. Someone added later is Staff, and an owner switches on what they can do, one switch per thing. They sign in with Google, Apple or an email code like any Norcoach login, no coach account needed. A reply in Support says who on staff wrote it.
