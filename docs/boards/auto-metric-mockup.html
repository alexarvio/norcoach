Check-in: metrics filled automatically

Today Steps looks like any other field, so nothing says Apple Health fills it, and you could type over it. The idea in all three: the figure stays, there is no field to type in, and one quiet mark says where it comes from. Weight and sleep stay typed (until the phone fills them too, then they look like Steps). The same mark shows in the check-in's Entries list.

In all three
  • No field and no underline on Steps: a tap does nothing. It counts as filled, so the "3 of 5 still to fill" line never asks for it.
  • The coach sees the same mark on the reading in their check-ins table, so they know it wasn't typed.
  • If the phone has no figure for the day yet (Health off, no steps), the row is an ordinary field again, so nothing is ever stuck empty.
