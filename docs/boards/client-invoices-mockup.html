A client's invoices

Alex's Invoices tab, for what was settled today: Norcoach takes no cut. Every invoice email (and Invoices in Alex's app) links to our own page, app.norcoachapp.com/invoice/…, with Pay going to Stripe's checkout on Finlay's own Stripe account (card, iDEAL, Apple Pay). On a repeating plan Alex can choose there to pay automatically from then on; later invoices are charged by themselves. A charge that fails is retried the next day, three days in a row, and only after the third is Finlay told; Alex's app is never locked for it. A bank transfer stays marked paid by hand. The tab today has no place for any of that: the plan is a rust strip, the series a separate table, the invoices a status menu with no story. Both options put the billing on top and give every invoice what happened to it.

What changes

  • The billing is on the screen. The rust strip (On Alex, €89 a month, not billed.) and the separate Recurring table become one Billing card: the plan, the price, the rhythm and next invoice, how Alex pays, its state pill. Not billed, it is the same card with Start billing as its one action.
  • Alex's own price. €75 with the plan's €89 struck beside it. In A the price is edited in place on the card (Save from the next invoice on); in B it sits in the ⋯. Edit lines changes the running series from its next invoice; sent invoices stay as they were.
  • Next number goes. It is a Settings fact, not one about Alex. Its place in the figures is Last paid, with how.
  • Every invoice tells what happened to it: sent, opened (app or browser), reminders, paid and how (card, iDEAL, Apple Pay), automatic payment turned on, a charge and its retries, marked paid by hand, refunded. A opens it on the row; B in the well under the track.
  • The status pill stops being a menu. It follows what happened (new: Charge failed, Refunded), and the actions are words in the opened row: Send link, Remind, Mark paid, Not paid, Refund. A slip in a menu can no longer mark something Paid.
  • A failed charge is drawn as it runs: declined, retry 1, 2 and 3 a day apart, the ones to come faint. Finlay is told (To do row and a push) only after the third; until then it is this tab's alone. Alex's app is never locked.
  • Start billing is a short pop-up in C (price, first invoice, how Alex pays: Link or Bank transfer) instead of the whole New invoice. + New invoice stays for one-offs.
  • A or B: A reads like every other log in the admin and scales to years of invoices; B shows the rhythm and a run of trouble at a glance but holds about six periods before it has to scroll.
