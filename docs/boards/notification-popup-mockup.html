Which open the pop-up

The push tapped, then every other notification that came in since the app was last open, newest after it, five at most. A message from Finlay never does: it opens the chat straight, as today. Read in the pop-up, a notification is read in the bell too.

The switches

A switch turns off the push to the phone. The bell keeps every notification either way, so nothing is lost. The always-on ones (sign-in codes, the app being ready, a workout left running) have no switch. With notifications blocked in the phone's own settings, the fold says Blocked in phone settings and nothing else.
