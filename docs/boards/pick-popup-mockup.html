Pick pop-ups: Duplicate which?

What the rules say now (§1, 17 Oct): a pop-up where you enter or pick things keeps its fields and tiles, white with a hairline, the chosen one ink, one ink pill to save. So today's tiles are inside the rules. But since then the gym drop-down and the swap's library went flat: rows on the page, no boxes. A pop-up that is only a list to pick from could read the same.

If you pick B or C
  • The rule would split pop-ups in two: forms (fields to type, choices to set) keep their white hairline boxes; a list to pick one from is flat rows, space between them, a chevron after each, no boxes. The same for Add a session's first step, the gym picker's list and Copy a meal.
  • Nothing else in the pop-up changes: the title at 600, the round ‹ and ✕, white, centred.
