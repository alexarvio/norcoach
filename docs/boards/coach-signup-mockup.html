The sign-in card with another title. Apple is off on the website, so a coach on a laptop sees Google and an email box.

A · The card, made a coach's

Today's white card and centred logo stay, but it now reads as a new coach account, not a login: Create your coach account, with the three steps (Account, About you, Approval) as a small line under the logo, green once done and ink for the one you're on. Departing from today's sign-in rule, the page carries Home's faint star and the laptop's card widens to show the whole way in at once, the steps down its left with a fact each and the form on its right.

B · The website carries on

The sign-up is the next page of the website the coach just left: its dark band, hero and founding perks beside the app's own form on paper, so Apply for early access lands on more of the same instead of a login wall. On the phone the band is a short head over each step, and once they are in the queue the paper goes and the band fills the screen with what a founding coach gets.

C · One question at a time

No card: each step is the page itself with one question on it, and it asks who they are first (name, then business) before how they sign in, so a coach coming from "Apply for early access" feels they are applying rather than meeting a login wall. One field a screen means the question, the field and Continue all stay in sight above the phone's keyboard, and the wait greets them by name.

D · See your app as you sign up

The sign-up shows the product: on About you, the client's phone fills in with the coach's name and business as they type, instead of a sentence saying how they'll appear. A coach who just saw the client's phone on the website watches that phone become theirs, while the first two steps stay the app's plain fields and pills, with no card.
