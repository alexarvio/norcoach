Workout as a sheet

The idea from Kahunas: the session as one page you scroll down, each exercise a small table of its sets, like a spreadsheet, but in our own look. No swiping between exercises, and nothing ever moves on by itself while you type: a set counts once you tick it or fill its last box, and you scroll on when you want to. The jump you hit today can't happen here, because there is no timer moving the screen.

A · Every exercise open, one long sheet Suggested

Every exercise is a block on one page: its name with the demo, chat and ⋯ beside it, the target as one grey line, then a row for each set with last time, kg, reps and a tick. Done rows go flat with a green check. The progress bar at the top lights as you scroll. Closest to Kahunas, and easy to read back at a glance.

B · Done exercises fold to one line

The same sheet, but an exercise you've finished folds down to one line (name, sets, best set) and the one you're on is open. The page stays short, so what you're doing is always near the top. A tap on a folded line opens it again to change something. A little less like a spreadsheet, a little calmer.

The PB moment

Three places, from the moment it happens to later. The phone needs each lift's best inside its rep range sent ahead, so it can tell on the spot, with no signal too. Today it only learns about a PB after the server has the set.

1 · On the set, as it's ticked

The tick turns into a green PB ring, the phone buzzes, and a small card under the exercise says what beat what. It goes away by itself, and if the number was a typo and gets changed, it goes with it.

My pick: A, with all three PB moments. A gives you the spreadsheet calm you liked, keeps every number in sight, and removes the jump completely. B is calmer if sessions get long. Building it is a real change to the workout (about a week of careful work: the logging logic, offline saving, last time, the far-off check, warm-ups and swaps all carry over; the pages, the swiping and the timers go). Until then I'd fix today's jump in the current screen right away: leaving a box never logs the grey numbers, and nothing moves on while you're typing.
