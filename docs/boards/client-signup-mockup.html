Client sign-up with a coach code

  • Client: create account (Apple, Google, or email + password) → confirm email (email only) → name and date of birth → coach code → waiting until the coach says yes. Phone and the rest come later.
  • Coach code: one per coach, their name by default (FINLAY), changeable. Also a link and QR (app.norcoachapp.com/join/finlay) that fills the code in, for an Instagram bio.
  • Coach: a push "Alex Arvio wants to join you", and a Join requests row in the Feed's Needs you. Accept makes them a client with their name, birthdate and email filled in; Decline lets them try another code.
  • A wrong code just says Not a valid code; the coach only shows once the code is someone's.
  • The coach's invite link stays, for coaches who add the client first. The code is for when the client finds the coach.
  • Coach sign-up: first and last name, business name. No date of birth: nothing uses it, and Stripe asks for its own when they get paid.

The client

The coach sign-up's dark head over the paper. The code shows who it is before sending, so a typo never lands with the wrong coach.

The motion

So it never jumps from screen to screen (18 Nov, Alex: "have a smooth transition"). Play it, or tap through.

  1. Between steps the dark head and the paper stay put: the title fades up into the new one, the form slides out to the left as the next slides in from the right (320ms, the fold's curve). Back slides the other way.
  2. The code: once it is a coach's, their picture, name and business fade in under it and settle 4px, and Send request turns ink. A wrong one: Not a valid code fades in in rust instead.
  3. Send request: the button's word becomes three soft dots while it goes, then the paper drops away down the screen, the coach's picture lifts out of its row and grows into its place on the dark, and Request sent, Waiting for Finlay, the facts and the buttons come in one after another.
  4. While waiting a faint ring breathes out of the picture: it's live.
  5. The yes (open or not, the push too): the ring turns green with a check, Waiting for Finlay becomes You're in, and Home rises over the dark like a sheet of paper.
  6. With reduced motion the screens simply change.
