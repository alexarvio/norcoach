3 · The steps

Thirteen. The rail first, then into Sam's tabs, then Settings, the chat and help.

1RailFeed

Feed

Your notification centre. Here you see what your clients are doing, and everything that needs your attention, such as messages to answer and videos to review.

2RailPhases

Phases

Every client's plan on one timeline. Here you see at a glance whose phase is ending and who needs a new one.

3RailCalendar

Calendar

Here you plan your week: calls with clients, one-to-one training sessions, and anything else of your own.

4RailLibrary

Library

Your exercises and their demo videos. You decide, client by client, whether they can watch a demo in their workout.

5RailNew client

Add a client

Here is where you add your clients.

6SamHome

Overview

An overview of each client on one screen: their phases, strength, weight and consistency, together with their meetings and invoices.

7SamPlan

Plan

Their training, nutrition and lifestyle phases on one timeline, together with their events, such as trips and holidays.

8SamLifestyle

Lifestyle

Deploy personalised metrics for your client to fill in each day, week or month, such as weight, sleep or steps, and collect their answers as they come in.

9SamTraining

Training

Here is where you create and deploy personalised programmes for every client's needs, week by week. Choose the columns you work with, and follow every set they log.

10SamNutrition

Nutrition

Here is where you set your client's nutritional targets, and which supplements they take, how much and when. You also see every meal they log in the app.

11RailSettings

Settings

Here is where you set up your profile and your business: your logo and brand colour, the Norcode your clients join you with, and your invoice details.

12EdgeChat

Chat

Here is where you chat with each of your clients.

13Edge?

Help

Here you find guides and tutorials for Norcoach, and you can contact our team whenever you have a question.

Sam, the example client

What he holds

  • Made for every new coach the first time they save Your business, in the background, so he is there by Open Norcoach. The coaches from before never get one.
  • Training: a 12-week Hypertrophy Block eight weeks in, Push, Pull, Legs and Upper, its sets logged at two gyms (as today's example builder; its exercises matched to the preset library's names).
  • Lifestyle: a lifestyle phase with weight, sleep and steps, two weeks of daily answers. New: today's builder puts them on no phase, so the tab would be empty.
  • Nutrition: a nutrition phase with targets, two supplements and a few days of meals. New: today's builder logs calories alone.
  • Plan: one event, a trip. Meetings: a past call with its summary and one coming up. Chat: a few messages.
  • No login, no emails, no pushes, no invoices, no bell notifications.

Where he never counts

  • A grey Example pill after his name in the rail and on his Home. A flag on the client, never his name.
  • Never in Business (clients, by month, stay, not billed), never offered when Accept asks where a join request goes, never a clash on the Calendar.
  • His Home's ⋯ has Delete Sam, asking first with no name to type, and no Open Sam's app.
Your call: should Sam owe one thing on purpose (an unread message), so Needs you, the To do and the rail's number have a row to show in the tour? Or should nothing of his ever count there?
