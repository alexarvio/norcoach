3 · The steps

Twelve. The rail first, then into Sam's tabs, then Settings, the chat and help. The last card says where Sam goes.

1
RailFeed

Feed

Needs you lists what is waiting on you: messages, videos to review, notes to read. Under it, Activity shows what your clients did, newest first.

2
RailPhases

Phases

Every client's phases on one timeline. The top lists who needs a new phase and whose phase ends soon.

3
RailCalendar

Calendar

Your client calls and your own appointments, in one calendar.

4
RailLibrary

Library

Your exercises and their demo videos. Clients can play the demo from the exercise in their workout.

5
RailNew client

Add a client

A first name is enough to start building their plan. Add an email to send them an invite, or share your Norcode so they can ask to join.

6
SamHome

Overview

Sam's phases, strength, weight and consistency on one screen, with everything he did underneath.

7
SamPlan

Phases and events

Sam's lifestyle, training and nutrition phases on one timeline, with his trips and other events under them.

8
SamLifestyle

Check-ins

You pick what Sam logs each day, week or month, like weight, sleep or steps. His answers come in here.

9
SamTraining

Programme

You write Sam's programme week by week, and every set he logs shows against your target. When he lifts heavier inside the rep range, the next weeks' target moves up to it.

10
SamNutrition

Targets and meals

Set Sam's calories, macros and supplements. Every meal he logs shows here.

11
RailSettings

Settings

Your profile and Norcode, your logo and brand colour, and your invoice details.

12
EdgeChat and ?

Chat and help

Every client's chat in one place, each message showing what it's about. The ? has short videos and a way to message us. When you're done with Sam, delete him from his Home.

Sam, the example client

What he holds

  • Made for every new coach the first time they save Your business, in the background, so he is there by Open Norcoach. The coaches from before never get one.
  • Training: a 12-week Hypertrophy Block eight weeks in, Push, Pull, Legs and Upper, its sets logged at two gyms (as today's example builder; its exercises matched to the preset library's names).
  • Lifestyle: a lifestyle phase with weight, sleep and steps, two weeks of daily answers. New: today's builder puts them on no phase, so the tab would be empty.
  • Nutrition: a nutrition phase with targets, two supplements and a few days of meals. New: today's builder logs calories alone.
  • Plan: one event, a trip. Meetings: a past call with its summary and one coming up. Chat: a few messages.
  • No login, no emails, no pushes, no invoices, no bell notifications.

Where he never counts

  • A grey Example pill after his name in the rail and on his Home. A flag on the client, never his name.
  • Never in Business (clients, by month, stay, not billed), never offered when Accept asks where a join request goes, never a clash on the Calendar.
  • His Home's ⋯ has Delete Sam, asking first with no name to type, and no Open Sam's app.
