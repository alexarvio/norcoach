The coach's brand in Norcoach

A little of the coach, not a new app: their colour on the one main button of a screen and the tab you are on, their name or banner where NORCOACH is, their logo behind Home and the chat as now. Everything else stays as it is: rust for what needs doing, green for done, the track colours. The brand only ever fills things (a pill, a bubble, the tab you are on), never a word, ring or line as rust and green do, so the coach can pick any colour; one too light is darkened until white reads on it. The coach here is made up, "Peak Form Coaching". Pick a colour or type one, and every screen below changes.

Brand colour

1 · How far the colour goes

Norcoach as it is today on the left, then the brand at two reaches. Both keep the top bar's words, toggles, the done check and every track colour as they are.

The difference
  • A: today, ink.
  • B · Light (my pick): the screen's one main pill (Start, Save, Join, Finish, Record) and the tab you are on. The faint logo behind Home takes the colour too, still faint. Most screens show it once or twice.
  • C · Light + chat: B, and the client's own bubbles and Send in the chat. The chat is where the coach is felt most, but it is a lot of colour on one screen.

2 · Where NORCOACH is

The top bar's word changes colour as you scroll: ink on the page, white over Training's and Nutrition's photos. So the banner is drawn in one colour, as the logo behind Home is, never in its own colours.

  • Banner: the coach uploads their wordmark (transparent, wide). Norcoach takes out its background and draws it in the bar's colour.
  • No banner: the business name in the bar's small caps, shrinking to fit.
  • Neither: NORCOACH, as today. Norcoach then stays only as a small line at the burger's foot (Norcoach), so the client can find support.

3 · The coach's admin

The coach sees their brand while they work: the rail's logo, the view they are on, and the one ink pill of a pop-up. State tags, rust and green stay as they are.

4 · Where the coach sets it

Settings, Business details, under Logo. Each part saves at once, as Logo does.

Settings, Business details

Banner beside Logo, then Brand colour: eight swatches as quick picks, and + for any colour at all. A colour too light for white words on it is darkened, keeping its hue, and it says so in one grey line (try a yellow or a pale blue above).

5 · The app icon: dropped (19 Nov, Alex: "then forget about it"; the icon stays the Norcoach star)

The iPhone and Android app is one app in the stores for every coach. It can only switch to icons built into it, iPhone shows its own line when it does ("You have changed the icon for Norcoach"), and the name under the icon stays Norcoach. How the others do it (checked 19 Nov): Everfit lets a coach pick from a list of its own icons ($75 once); Trainerize Pro puts the coach's own icon into its shared iPhone app ($169 once, weeks); a coach's own name under the icon comes only with an app of their own in the stores, under their own Apple account.
