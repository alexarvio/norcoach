What the audit means, in pictures

Every high and medium finding (91), each as how it looks now and how it would look fixed, with one plain sentence. High first in each area. Nothing has been changed yet.

Across every page 4

1highOn an iPad, some rows have no ⋯ menu at all

On a touch screen there is no hover, so on Lifestyle, Supplements, Invoices, Plans and Library cards the ⋯ never shows up and you can't rename or delete anything; Training's shows at 70%. After the fix every row shows its ⋯ in full on a touch screen.

2mediumThe phase switcher's Live | Draft toggle is smaller

The Live / Scheduled / Draft / Past toggle in the phase menu is shorter, with tiny capital letters, so it looks different from every other toggle in the admin (like KG | LBS); after the fix it is the same size with the same words.

3mediumCalls on Meetings snap open instead of sliding

Clicking a call on Meetings (or a coach's thread in the Support inbox) makes its notes appear in one jump, while Training sessions and check-ins slide open smoothly; after the fix they all slide open the same way.

4mediumSearch boxes come in six different shapes

The search boxes on the rail, Training, Substitutes and the chat list are all different heights, corners and edges, so the same kind of box looks different on every screen; after the fix they are all the rail's slim pill with a dark edge.

Left menu, Feed, Phases, Business 16

5highEvery coach sees Full Potential under the logo

The line under the logo is fixed to Finlay's business plus a tagline, so every other coach sees someone else's name. It should be their own business name and nothing more.

6highEnding soon's card edge is a different grey

The card around Ending soon gets its edge from the client app, a flatter grey than every other admin card. It should have the same thin line as the Needs you card above it.

7mediumThe track icons over Ending soon are heavier

On the Phases screen, the same small Training icon has a thin line over Needs you and a thicker one over Ending soon. Both should use the thin line.

8mediumThe Feed's action buttons have icons of different weights

In the Needs you column, the tick and the plus are drawn bolder than the play, lock and pen next to them. All of them should use one line weight.

9mediumLibrary's icon in the menu is squashed and thin

The menu icons come from different sets, so Coaches has a slightly thinner line and Library's dumbbell is squashed short. Drawn the same way, they match in size and weight.

10mediumNothing in the menu lines up on the left

The logo, the view icons, the Clients label, the search glass and the faces each start a different distance from the edge (16, 18, 22 or 24px). They should all start on one line.

11mediumA client's initial looks different on each screen

When a client has no photo, the round initial changes colour, weight and even fill between the menu, the Feed, Phases and the event pop-up. It should be the same white ring with a dark initial everywhere.

12mediumThe welcome tour describes an old menu

The tour says Feed's number counts clients and leaves out Library. Its card also has smaller corners and different buttons from every other pop-up.

13mediumPhases spaces its sections unevenly

On Phases there are 46px between Needs you and Ending soon and 28px before the clients, while Feed and Business use 22px everywhere.

14mediumPhases' empty box is a different size

With no clients, Phases puts its words further in and makes the box taller than the empty box everywhere else.

15mediumThe All clients pill changes weight when you pick

The client filter is grey and thin until you pick someone, then turns dark and bold, and it sits further from the toggle than other controls do. It should be dark and bold from the start, 8px from the toggle.

16mediumClient names are three different sizes

The same name is 14.5px in Needs you, 14px in Activity and 15px on Business. It should be one size everywhere.

17mediumThe page numbers sit in square boxes

The page numbers are boxed with small square corners in a third shade of grey, unlike the round buttons with no outline nearby. They should be plain round buttons, with the current page as a dark circle.

18mediumDrops on Business show in two different reds

The drop in Clients is rust, but the drop in Money by month is a brighter red on the same page. Both should be rust.

19mediumOne unpaid invoice reads Open in one place, Sent in another

The month pop-up calls an unpaid invoice Open in rust, while Owed calls the same invoice Sent in black. Both should show its real state.

20mediumNot billed rows have no date or face

Owed rows start with a date and the client's face, but the Not billed rows below start with just the name. They should start the same way, so the names line up.

A client's Home and Plan 11

21highThe same icons are drawn thicker on the Plan

The heart, dumbbell and apple have thinner lines on Home than on the Plan and its pop-ups, so the same icon looks heavier when you switch tabs; after, they match.

22mediumThe small word over the name changes between Home and Plan

The small HOME and PLAN labels over the client's name differ in grey and spacing, so the header seems to shift as you switch tabs; after, both labels are the same.

23mediumThe Plan list in Details looks unlike every other menu

The list of coaching plans in Details has a rounder card with an outline and lighter, taller rows than every other menu in the admin; after, it looks like the others.

24mediumThe Gender switch nudges sideways when you pick

In Details the picked gender turns bold while the others stay light, so the switch changes width a little with each pick; after, every word is bold all the time and nothing moves.

25mediumA client without a photo gets six different circles

When a client has no photo, their initial circle is white in some places and grey in others, with the letter in black or grey, bold or light; after, it is one white circle with a grey bold letter everywhere.

26mediumThe Move pop-up is narrower than the other phase pop-ups

After you drag a phase or an event, the Move pop-up opens narrower than the other phase and event pop-ups; after, they are all the same width (drawn here at half size).

27mediumAn open event doesn't say if it's on now or coming up

When you open an event on the Plan you see only its category, with a faint outlined Past on old ones; after, it carries the same coloured Live, Scheduled or Past tag that phases have.

28mediumTwo styles of small labels in the same pop-up

In the event and phase pop-ups, the Starts and Ends labels are smaller and paler than the When label right above them; after, all the small labels match.

29mediumThe to-do strip and the Feed's Needs you don't match

The rust to-do strip on a client's tab has shorter rows and plain action icons, while the Feed's Needs you has taller rows and white round buttons; after, the strip looks exactly like the Feed.

30mediumDelete is a different red in the menu

Delete in the ⋯ menu is a darker red than the Delete in the Details pop-up and on its confirm button; after, all three use the same red.

31mediumThe chat's client search looks unlike the other searches

Search clients in the chat panel is a faint outlined box with no magnifier, while the searches in the side menu and the Library have a dark outline and a magnifier; after, it looks like them.

Training 21

32high"+ Custom column" does nothing when clicked

The Columns menu offers a Custom column that only pops up a 'not wired yet' note, so it goes until it really works.

33highExercise names turn navy blue under the mouse

Hovering an exercise name in an open session turns it navy with a blue underline, while a session name turns ink with a grey one; both should be ink.

34highThe ? help buttons on Training turn navy

The little ? beside KG | LBS and on the week rail is drawn darker than on Home and Settings and turns navy under the mouse; it should match Home's soft grey that turns ink.

35highThe note mark in the ? legend is a blue pill

The legend should show the note exactly as it appears on a session row, a thin grey ring with the pen, not a blue capsule the coach never sees anywhere else.

36mediumCardio rows end in two square buttons, not the ⋯

Exercise rows end in one round ⋯ menu, but cardio rows (and new rows) show a dumbbell with a bold count and a bin as squarish buttons; they should all end in the same ⋯.

37mediumAfter-workout answers use "Show all" instead of pages

The answered workouts sit in shorter rows with a line and a Show all button under them, while every other log has taller rows and the page numbers under the card.

38mediumAfter-workout check title changes size and place

With no questions the title is big above the box; add one question and it shrinks and jumps inside the card, so it should stay in one place and size.

39mediumExercises | Cardio words get bolder when picked

In the Columns menu the word you pick turns bolder and the other gets thinner, so the words shift; every other toggle keeps both words equally bold.

40mediumA changed exercise row flashes a faint blue

While a changed figure is saving, its row turns a faint blue for a moment; rows should never get a tinted highlight.

41mediumThe gym picker in exercise progress looks different

The gym list in an exercise's progress pop-up has an outline, rounder corners and thinner grey words, unlike every other dropdown, which is edgeless with bold ink words.

42mediumMuscle map's arrow flips faster and stiffer

When you open the Muscle map (or the exercise search) its little arrow snaps round in half the time, while a session's arrow turns slowly and softly; they should turn the same way.

43mediumIcons in the ? legend and pop-ups look heavier

In the ? legend and Training's pop-ups the video, photo and swap marks are drawn with thicker lines than the same marks on the rows they explain; they should be the same thin line.

44mediumTiny bold capitals in the + Week menu

The grey fact on the right of each menu choice should be quiet ordinary words, not a tiny bold all-caps tag.

45mediumThe muscle map pops open instead of easing

Clicking Muscle map should slide it open smoothly, the way a session now opens on the same screen, instead of the body drawings appearing in one jump.

46mediumLines between rows are three different greys

The opened session, the after-workout check and the progress log each draw the line between rows in a slightly different grey, and they should all use the same one.

47mediumEvery save on Training is announced twice

The tab already says All changes saved after its title, so the extra Saved pop-up that appears at the bottom after reordering or adding a session should go.

48mediumThree different switches on one tab

The demo switch, Ask every session and the substitutes switch should all be the same size and grey and slide the same smooth way.

49mediumCouldn't-train and swap notes sit in coloured boxes

The client's reason for skipping should read like their other notes, a pen and their words in quotes, and the swap line should sit flat with just its amber ring, not in a peach or amber box.

50mediumLast session and 30 days figures are oversized

The change figures in an opened session show at the page's large plain size, and they should be small, semi-bold and evenly spaced like the other figures.

51mediumA bluish ring when hovering a progress figure

Pointing at a Last session or 30 days figure draws a blue-grey ring, and it should be the same neutral grey used everywhere else.

52mediumOne half-done session, two names and two colours

A session the client started but didn't finish shows as a rust Unfinished on its row and as a soft green Started, not finished on the week card, and it should be one word and one colour.

Lifestyle, Nutrition, Shape update 12

53highYou can't reply to a client's food-day note

On Nutrition a client's note shows as bare quoted words, while on Lifestyle the same note says who wrote it and has Reply under it, so you can answer straight from the row.

54mediumA food day jumps open instead of sliding

Opening a day on Nutrition jumps straight from shut to fully open, while a Lifestyle check-in slides open smoothly over about a third of a second.

55mediumA past shape update jumps open instead of sliding

Opening a row under All shape updates snaps its photos in at once; it should slide open and fade in the same way a Lifestyle check-in does.

56mediumOpened rows are shaded three different ways

An opened row turns grey in one place on Shape update, only its top line on Nutrition, and not at all on Lifestyle; they should all look the same, plain white like Lifestyle.

57mediumAn empty Supplements card has no words and is the wrong size

With no supplements the card shows only a loose + Item at its top left; it should be the same empty box as everywhere else: None yet on the left and the + Item pill on the right.

58mediumTwo empty boxes on Lifestyle have different buttons

No phase yet has a black button and None yet has a white one a touch shorter; the same empty box should always have the same button.

59mediumThe 'nothing here' dash is four different greys

An empty value is shown in a different grey on each table, and Nutrition's figures use a shorter dash; they should all be one grey and one dash.

60mediumYour comment on a meal sits in a grey box

Under a meal your comment is in a grey box with a cramped label, while the same comment in the photo pop-up and on Shape update is plain text on the page.

61mediumLifestyle's group tags are too strongly coloured

The Group tags on tracked metrics are filled about twice as strong as every other coloured tag in the admin, so a column of them reads as coloured blocks.

62mediumThe same check-in has two dates, Table vs Graph

A weekly check-in reads Mon, 12 Oct in the Table but Week 42 on the Graph and its hover label; both should use the Table's date.

63mediumShape update names are as light as the date

In All shape updates the name is the same light weight as the date in front of it, so nothing stands out; it should be bold like a session's name on Training, and the weigh-in bold like the other logs' figures.

64mediumThe 'delete photos too' tick looks unlike other pop-ups

Removing an angle with photos shows a bare tick box with a heavy check and reads as an extra; it should sit in the soft grey box other pop-ups use and say what you accept: I understand the photos are deleted.

Meetings, Invoices, Calendar 12

65highMark completed has no Cancel, just a long sentence

The grey button at the bottom says 'Complete without one' where every other pop-up says Cancel, so there's no plain way out; after the fix it reads Cancel next to Complete, and Complete works with an empty summary.

66highThe invoice page still has the old navy buttons

Opening an invoice shows square-cornered boxes, navy words, a navy filled button and a pink warning block on a different grey; after the fix it matches the rest of the admin, with a plain back link, a black pill and the warning as a short rust line with its button.

67mediumEdit link puts red Remove where Save should be

Clear the link and the black Save turns into red Remove on the right, so nothing looks like the main button any more; after the fix Remove sits on the left in red and Save stays on the right.

68mediumThe invoice page explains itself in a long sentence

Above the invoice, a sentence explains how sending works and the button says 'Print or save as PDF'; after the fix it's just a Not sent tag and a Print button.

69mediumThe timezone list looks unlike every other dropdown

The timezone list has rounder corners, a grey outline, a softer shadow and thinner, smaller rows than every other dropdown; after the fix it looks like the ⋯ menus.

70mediumThree dropdowns in one pop-up, three different looks

In Schedule a meeting, the Time list has taller, thinner rows with a black picked row, while the Repeat list beside it has bolder rows with a grey picked row; after the fix both lists use the same rows and the same black pick.

71mediumLabels in Schedule a meeting come in three sizes

TOPIC, REPEAT and NOW are each a slightly different size, spacing and grey in the same pop-up; after the fix all three are the same size and the same grey.

72mediumPast calls draw the call icon thinner than Activity does

Past calls use their own copy of the call icon with a slightly thinner line than the same icon in Activity and the Feed; after the fix both use the same icon at the same weight.

73mediumMeetings pops up a 'Saved' message other tabs don't

After you schedule, move or complete a call, a 'Saved' box slides up in the corner, while every other tab only shows its quiet 'All changes saved' next to the title; after the fix Meetings works the same way.

74mediumA new calendar entry gets a dot in its name

Pick a client without typing a title and the entry is named 'Client call · Anna', so a typed dot shows on the calendar and in Meetings; after the fix it reads 'Call with Anna'.

75mediumTwo different reds for the same Delete

Delete in the ⋯ menu is a darker red than Delete at the bottom of the pop-up it opens, one click later; after the fix both are the same red.

76mediumPast call titles start further right than other logs

Past calls give the date a fixed wide column, so call titles start further right than titles in Activity and the other logs; after the fix the column is only as wide as the dates and the titles line up.

Settings, Library, Coaches 15

77highSettings never tells you a save failed

On Settings the little message that pops up after Save (Saved, or Couldn't save) never appears, so a failed save goes unnoticed; the fix makes it show, as it already does on Library and Coaches.

78highThe Published switch looks different from the other switches

Turned off, the Published switch is a filled grey blob with a shadowed knob, while every other switch in Settings is white with a thin edge; after the fix it matches them, and it moves the same smooth way too.

79mediumDevices list uses a typed | between facts

Under each device the facts are split by a typed | that reads faint and thin; after the fix they get the short drawn bar used everywhere else in the admin.

80mediumExport row uses a typed | between facts

The Export line under Your data splits its facts with typed | marks; after the fix they get the same short drawn bar as the rest of the admin.

81mediumDevices says when they were used in different words

The Devices list writes '3 days ago' while the chat writes 'Active 13/11' for the same kind of fact (and counts 'now' as 5 minutes instead of 2); after the fix both say it the chat's way.

82mediumTwo Replace buttons, two sizes, one page

The Replace button on your profile photos is a little shorter than the Replace button on your logo; after the fix both are the same pill.

83mediumRed word buttons come in three sizes

Remove on the logo, Disconnect in Settings and Decline on Coaches are each a different height and only one lights up under the mouse; after the fix they are one size with one hover.

84mediumThe Connected pills don't line up row to row

Stripe puts Disconnect before its Connected pill and Google puts it after, so the pills sit in different places down the page; after the fix every row has the same order and the pills line up.

85mediumSettings has no chat tab and runs wider

Every other coach page has the chat tab on its right edge with room kept for it; Settings has neither, so its content stretches further right; after the fix it matches.

86mediumPop-up messages use off-shade green and red

The little Saved and Couldn't save messages use a green and a red that are slightly off from the app's own, a bluish shadow and a thin title; after the fix they use the app's green and red, a neutral shadow and the normal bold title.

87mediumDelete in the ⋯ menus is a different red

Delete in the ⋯ menus is a darker, browner red than the red Disconnect and Remove words next to it; after the fix every red action is the same red.

88mediumThe address fields come in a different order each time

Your business address asks Street, City, Region, Postcode, but a client's address asks Address, Postcode, City, Country (and your own details mix it again); after the fix every address form asks the same fields in the same order with the same names.

89mediumPublic profile has its own Saved message

The bottom of your public profile says 'Saved | 2 min ago' or 'Not saved yet' in its own grey words, a second save message unlike the one the rest of the admin uses; after the fix only the Published switch stays there.

90mediumNew client's Member info | App access jumps

In the New client pop-up the picked tab is bolder than the other and the dark pill jumps instead of sliding; after the fix both words keep one weight and the pill glides across like every other toggle.

91mediumVideo upload promises 200 MB but stops at 128

The Add video box says files up to 200 MB are fine, then a 150 MB clip is refused for being over 128 MB; after the fix the box says the real limit.
