Website: the why

Every changed line on the site, now beside the proposed one. The proposed copy is the stronger of two drafts, or a merge of them: A (the ceiling) and B (the chain). Each line says why it matters to a one-to-one coach stuck working in his business, and no card is longer than the one it replaces. Revised after a cold read as the persona: each why said once, no slogans, and the chores that go away named. Counts include alt text and the mocks’ words, as the site audit counted them. Nothing on the site has changed yet.

The why, in five sentences
  1. A one-to-one coach grows when clients feel coached by him: they stay longer, send him their friends and are happy to pay for it.
  2. Past about twenty clients, every new one costs another evening in spreadsheets and a little of the quality they pay for, so he ends up working in his business instead of on it.
  3. Norcoach brings every client into one place, with one list of who needs him, and the sums and the check-in reminders done for him.
  4. His clients get a proper app from their first day, planned in phases they can see, week 4 of 6 and the block after it, with his face and name on everything and nothing written by AI.
  5. Better coaching keeps clients and brings new ones, and the time he gets back goes on more clients or on finding them.
