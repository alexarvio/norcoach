How it works · Lifestyle

On your laptop
Now

Choose what each client checks in on, depending on their goals: sleep, steps, weight, how they feel, daily, weekly or monthly. Their answers come back to you as data, so you see how they’re doing between calls.

Proposed

Decide what each client checks in on, based on their goal, such as their weight, sleep, steps or how they feel, and whether they answer each day, week or month. Every answer comes back to you as data, gathered over time, so you can spot a trend long before it becomes a problem.

On their phone
Now

They check in with a few taps, and connect Apple Health or Health Connect so steps, weight and sleep fill in by themselves.

Proposed

Your client finds their check-in waiting on their home screen and fills it in with a few taps. If they connect Apple Health or Health Connect, their steps, weight and sleep fill in by themselves.

How it works · Training

On your laptop
Now

Build and send each client a workout plan made for them. Every session they log comes back to you, so you see at a glance who is progressing and who is stalling.

Proposed

Write each client a programme of their own, week by week, and it appears on their phone. Every set they log comes straight back to you, and Norcoach shows you who is getting stronger and whose weights have stalled.

On their phone
Now

They train with the workout screen: your notes on every exercise, what they lifted last time beside each set, and whether they beat it as they log. They can send you a video of a set for feedback, and every set saves even with no signal.

Proposed

Your client trains from your programme, with your notes on every exercise and what they lifted last time beside each set, so they always know what to beat. They can film a set and send it to you for feedback, and every set they log is kept, even with no signal in the gym.

How it works · Nutrition

On your laptop
Now

Set each client’s calories, macros and supplements, and review the meals they log.

Proposed

Set each client’s calories, macros and supplements for the phase they’re in. You can look through the meals they log, day by day, and leave a comment wherever it helps.

On their phone
Now

Their targets shown as rings. They browse the food library, build meals and send them to you to review.

Proposed

Your client sees their targets as rings that fill up through the day. They log their meals from a food library, add a photo of the plate, and save the meals they eat often so they’re one tap away next time.
