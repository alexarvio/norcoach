Coach sign-up in the browser

  • One flow, one look: every step in the sign-up's dark-and-paper frame, today's white plan card and the old welcome steps folded in. Each thing asked once (the name and business were asked twice).
  • Order: Account → Confirm email → About you (photo, first and last name, what you coach) → Your business (business name, country, time zone) → You’re set, with their code to share.
  • No plan step: the first coaches are free (18 Nov, Alex: no plans settled yet). When plans are, a plan step can go in before You’re set.
  • On a laptop the dark side lists the steps once they’re past the first: done ticked green, the one on screen white. On a phone, the step’s name in the dark head as now.
  • Left for Settings later: address, invoicing, payouts, the public profile.

The steps

A laptop each; the phone the same form under its dark head.

The motion

The same as the client sign-up: nothing jumps from screen to screen.

  1. After the first step the pitch on the dark side fades up and away and the list of steps comes in; each step done ticks green with a small pop.
  2. Between steps the form slides out to the left as the next slides in, the dark side still.
  3. You’re set: the code, the link and Open Norcoach come in one after another.
  4. Open Norcoach: the paper covers everything, the star, then the word from behind it, the admin’s opening.
