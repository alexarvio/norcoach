Proposal

Proposal

One rule

A switch turns off the push. The bell keeps the record, as Messages from {Coach} does today. Nothing the coach sent ever goes missing from the app.

Client · Settings · Notifications

  • Notifications on this phone first, then one switch per group: Messages from {Coach}, Training, Nutrition, Check-ins, Shape updates, Calls, Plan and events, Invoices. All on by default.
  • Calls covers the day-before and hour-before reminders.
  • Workout still going: the rules say it has no Settings row. Keep it always on, or let it ride on Training. Your call.
  • Check-in reminders: either make the due reminders real pushes under Check-ins, or drop the switch.

Coach · Settings · Notifications

  • Keep Messages from clients and Videos to review; add Join requests, Calls asked for, Events from clients, Payments. Same record on the laptop and the phone.
  • Quiet hours over all of them, Payments too. Decide: drop what comes in quiet hours (today) or send it when they end.
  • Needs you, the rail counts and dots stay as they are: they are the to-do list.

Found while mapping

  • A video reply from the review pop-up pushes twice: "replied to your video" and "Message from {Coach}". The Training tab's Reply pushes once.
  • A locked client's first phase going live pushes twice: "Your app is ready" and "New program from {Coach}".
  • Push titles: "New program from {Coach}" for nutrition and lifestyle; everything else "Update from {Coach}".
  • Push titles use the coach's full display name ("Update from Finlay Smith"), against DESIGN-RULES §8's first name; only the body swaps in the first name. The body is the stored line, starting with a bare verb and no subject: "Sent you invoice INV-12", "Cancelled your call", "Scheduled a meeting: "Topic"", "Set what to focus on: a · b" (middle dots).
  • A nutrition phase going live reads two ways: "set new nutrition targets. Check them out" (made live) and "set a new nutrition phase" (scheduled with its start already come).
  • A training phase with a draft programme is announced with the programme's words, "published a new plan", not the phase's.
  • A moved call reads three ways (Meetings' one call "Monday 16 Nov", a series "Mon 16 Nov", the Calendar "Moved your call"), and its push keeps "GMT+1".
  • Call reminders use a middle dot and promise a link that may not exist.
  • Time for your shape update, a new lifestyle phase and the old goals show as the grey system "Update".
  • No client push respects quiet hours. Three can come around midnight: the workout reminder, a scheduled programme going live (the every-request job, applyDueProgramDeployments, queries.ts:1261, on the first page load after its Monday 00:00) and a recurring invoice (runRecurringInvoices every 10 min, instrumentation.ts:129, at 00:00–00:10 on its issue day). They decide whether the client needs quiet hours of their own.
  • Built but shown nowhere: overdue invoice, missed check-ins, missed sessions, programme ending, nothing logged.
