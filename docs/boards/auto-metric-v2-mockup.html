Check-in: what you fill vs what the phone fills

The aim: at a glance you see which rows want your input and which are already taken care of. Weight, sleep and adherence are yours; Steps comes from Apple Health. Four ways, from the gentlest to the strongest. In every one the automatic row can't be typed in, counts as filled, and turns into an ordinary field on a day the phone has nothing.

Notes
  • A is your idea: the row faded, its figure on the right where every figure is, the lock in the unit's place on the right.
  • B is the clearest: the form is only what you fill, and the phone's figures sit apart at the foot, read-only. With several (steps, sleep, heart rate) they stack in that one block.
  • The heart is Apple Health's mark in spirit (not Apple's logo): red, small. Health Connect on Android would get its own.
