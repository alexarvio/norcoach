New invoice, made from the plan

The pop-up on a client's Invoices tab. Today the coach retypes what the plan already says, types {month} by hand, and picks Draft | Sent to Alex without knowing what Alex gets or how he pays. Here the plan fills the invoice, the month is a chip that reads itself, the repeat is said back in plain facts, and how Alex pays is a choice of its own: Pay by link opens our page at app.norcoachapp.com/invoice/… with Pay, which goes to Stripe on Finlay's own account (Norcoach takes no cut); on a repeat Alex can say yes to being charged by himself from then on. Both options are live: pick a plan or Custom, switch the repeat and how Alex pays, change the price.

What changes

  • Plan leads the pop-up, Alex's own picked from the start (the floating list of Finlay's plans with their price and rhythm, Custom at its foot). A plan fills the line, the price and the repeat; Custom clears them to one empty line and Once. Today only Start billing fills from the plan, and the coach types the rest.
  • A price changed on a plan line is a custom price for Alex: the plan's row says it, the plan's price struck and his in ink (€89 €80), and the series keeps his.
  • The month is a chip in the line, not {month} typed by hand: it reads November now and the month of each invoice the series makes; + Month on the line's hover adds it to any line, its × on hover takes it out.
  • The repeat is said back under its capsule in facts, €89 on the 26th of every monthnext 26/12, where today a grey next 26/12 sits in the label. On Once the line's room is kept, so nothing under it moves.
  • How Alex pays is new: Pay by link (card, iDEAL, Apple Pay on Finlay's own Stripe) or Bank transfer (Finlay marks it paid by hand, as today). On a repeat paid by link, Charge automatically: Alex is asked on his first payment, and the next invoices of the series are charged by themselves. A failed charge is tried again the next day, three days in a row; after the third Finlay is told on his To do, and Alex's app is never locked for it (that belongs to the Invoices tab, not this pop-up).
  • Draft | Sent to Alex goes. The footer names what happens: Send (the brand pill) sends it now and every one the series makes by itself; Save draft in words keeps it, and the series, waiting for Finlay.
  • The note starts empty: today's grey "Thank you" sample goes (Settings, no sample text).
  • B adds what Alex gets, the email or the page, drawn as it is typed. A shows nothing of it, so the pop-up stays 640 wide as every .dlh.
  • Not drawn here, for the Invoices tab itself: payment reminders, changing a running series (its price or lines from the next invoice on), refunds coming back.
