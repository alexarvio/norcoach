Plans, redesigned

Settings › Plans read like a spreadsheet: a name, a price, a rhythm and a count. Here each plan shows who is on it, what it brings in a month and what the price holds in VAT, and opening a plan lists its clients, where one client's own price is typed. Change a plan's price and it asks whether the clients already on it move too, from their next invoice. Everything on the board is clickable: open a plan, change its price, tick the box, Save, ⋯, + Plan.

A · Plans as cards

A card a plan, two across: its price big, the VAT in it grey, its clients' faces and what they bring in a month. A click opens the card where it is (the card under it moves down, nothing beside it): its name, price and rhythm to change, then its clients, each with their price; a figure typed that isn't the plan's makes it their own price, and the hover on that row offers Back to the plan's. The dashed tile is + Plan: it turns into the new card in its own room, as tall as the tile. Drawn with Online coaching open and its price typed up to €95.

What changes

  • Who is on a plan: their faces and the count, on the plan itself, and the list of them once it is opened (today: `1 client`).
  • What it brings in: `3 clients | €257 a month`, what the clients on it really pay, a 2-weekly plan counted as its month's worth (`€35` × 26 ÷ 12 = `€76 a month`), whole euros as Business counts.
  • The VAT in a price, grey under it, read from the business's rate (`€89` = `€73.55 + €15.45 VAT`), live while a price is typed.
  • A client's own price, typed on their row inside the plan; the plan says `1 on their own price`, the row `Own price`, and the row's hover `Back to €89`. Their invoices from the next one on carry it.
  • A new price on a plan is still for new clients first, as today. Typed, a grey well asks to Also move the clients on the old price, from their next invoice; a client on their own price never moves. Unticked, the old price becomes theirs, and the list says so before Save.
  • Adding: A the dashed last tile, turning into the card in its own room; B the row at the card's foot as today. Both keep + Plan beside the title, as the Library does.
  • As today: archived plans last and faded (out of the Details picker, their clients still billed), Delete in the ⋯ only with no client on it, asking first (here in the ⋯ itself), each change saved at once, never on the page's bar.
  • No Saved toast any more (today's code toasts `Saved` and `{Plan} added`, which the admin rules took out on 16 Nov): the card or row showing the new figures says it.
  • Not on this screen, on purpose: how each client pays (card, iDEAL, bank transfer) and a failed charge. They are the client's Invoices tab's and the invoice's.
