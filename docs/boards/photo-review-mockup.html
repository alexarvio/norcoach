Reviewing a photo or video, in today's style

One panel instead of two modes. Who sent it with their face; the exercise as the title; the facts in words (Week 6, never WK 6); their sets flat. At the foot, always: write a reply, or record one with the mic and camera beside Record. No keyboard legend, no photo count, no time, no words inside the record button.

What changes from today
  • No two modes. Today the panel is either "talk" (a reply box and a Record review button) or "record" (the big red button, Mic on, Camera off). Now it is one panel, the reply always at the foot.
  • A written reply goes to the client as the photo sent back to them with your words under it, and with whatever you drew on it. The exercise's mark goes green, as a recorded reply does.
  • Gone: the letter keys legend (P pen, A arrow…), "1 photo", the time, Sent unasked / You asked for this, the READY pill, and the sentence in the record box. The keys still work.
  • Kept: the tools on the dark stage, drawing before or while recording, the client's own words on it when they wrote any.
