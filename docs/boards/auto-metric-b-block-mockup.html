B: the "From Apple Health" block, lined up

The block under the list, three ways of lining it up. In all three the phone's numbers end on the same line as the form's numbers (90.3, 7), and their units sit in the same narrow column as kg and h, so the right edge reads as one column all the way down. The dashed rust lines show the shared edges.

My pick: 3. The names in the block start on the same left edge as Weight and Hours of sleep (and the heart with them), the numbers end on the form's number edge, the units in the form's unit column. The grey only frames them; it reaches a little past the form on both sides, so nothing inside has to move in to fit it.
