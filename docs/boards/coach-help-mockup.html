Help for coaches: guides and contact us

Two things in one place: guides (short videos with steps, such as "Add your first client", "Build a workout", "Onboard a client with your code") and contact us (the chat with the Norcoach team that already sits in Settings, Support, with Report a problem). Three ways to place it in the admin. The guides and the chat look the same in each; what changes is where you find them.

AHelp, a screen of its own in the menu

A Help row in the left menu, under Settings. The page is Guides | Contact us: the guides as video cards grouped by topic (Getting started, Clients, Training, Nutrition, Check-ins, Billing), a search on top, a tick on the ones already watched; Contact us is today's chat with the team, with Report a problem. Clear and easy to find; one more row in the menu.

In all three, a guide opens on its video (one you upload, or a YouTube or Vimeo link, as the Library's demos), then its steps in a few short lines; a guide can be linked in the chat. Contact us is the chat that exists today (Settings, Support), landing in your Coaches screen's Support inbox, with Report a problem and a screenshot.

Making the guides: you add them yourself, from the owner's Coaches screen, in a new Guides tab: a title, a topic, the video (upload or link), the steps, and which screen it belongs to (for B's "For this screen"). New guides show to every coach at once.
